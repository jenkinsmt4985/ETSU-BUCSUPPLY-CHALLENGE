First: what is a supply chain?

A supply chain is the path a product takes from suppliers, through a factory and warehouse, to the customer. Supply chain managers make sure the right products are available at the right time without wasting too much money or inventory.

The key lesson

You never knew demand perfectly. Your job was to make a reasonable plan, watch the outcome, and improve the next round. That is exactly why supply chain managers use forecasts, inventory, capacity, and simulations.

What do supply chain professionals do?

They solve problems like this every day: How much will customers want? How much should we order? How much can we make? How much inventory should we keep? How do we improve after something unexpected happens?
